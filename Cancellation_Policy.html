Cancellation Policy

Terrain Vanabas Resort

At Terrain Vanabas Resort, we understand that travel plans can change unexpectedly. Our cancellation policy is designed to be fair and transparent while ensuring smooth resort operations.

Standard Cancellation Terms
Free Cancellation Period

Guests may cancel their reservation free of charge up to 48 hours (2 days) before the scheduled check-in date. Full refund will be processed to the original payment method within 7-10 business days.

Important: You must cancel at least 2 days before your arrival date to receive a full refund.

Late Cancellation Fee

Cancellations made less than 48 hours before check-in will incur a cancellation fee equivalent to one night's room rate. The remaining amount will be refunded to your original payment method.

No-Show Policy

If you do not arrive and have not canceled your reservation, the total cost of the first night will be charged to your payment method. No refund will be issued for no-shows.

How to Cancel Your Booking

To cancel or modify your reservation, please contact us at least 2 days before your check-in date through any of the following methods:

  • Email: info@jcktpic.com (include your booking confirmation number)
  • Phone: Contact our reservations team directly
  • Original Booking Channel: Cancel through the platform you used to book
Special Circumstances

We recognize that emergencies happen. Exceptions to our cancellation policy may be granted for:

  • Medical emergencies (documentation required)
  • Natural disasters or extreme weather conditions
  • Government-imposed travel restrictions

Please contact our reservations team directly to discuss special circumstances. Each case will be reviewed individually by management.

Modification Policy

Changes to your reservation dates or room type are subject to availability. Modifications requested at least 48 hours before check-in can be made without charge. Modifications after this period may be subject to cancellation fees and rate differences.

Non-Refundable Bookings

Special promotional rates may be offered as non-refundable bookings. These reservations cannot be canceled, modified, or refunded under any circumstances. The non-refundable nature will be clearly indicated at the time of booking.

Please Note: Always check if your booking rate is refundable or non-refundable before confirming your reservation.

Acknowledgment:

By completing your booking, you acknowledge that you have read and agree to our cancellation policy. We recommend purchasing travel insurance for additional protection against unforeseen circumstances.
